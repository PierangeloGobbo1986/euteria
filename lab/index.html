1 · Open a song

A .euteria file from EUTERIA (Python), "Export for Webapp version". A full .musicproj works too, but it is much bigger. The sync tests open a small built-in project whose tracks all click at exactly 1.000 s and check where each click lands: "Sync test" uses the format of EUTERIA 4.3 (every track at 48 kHz), "old format" the one of 4.2 (Original Track at 44.1 kHz). The Original Track is lined up with the stems automatically.

3 · Export MP3

Exports the stems that are switched on, as they sound now (volumes, tempo, key), with the EUTERIA name: Song_BassDrumsVocals.mp3. Every export is added to the list below: tick the ones you want, then download them as separate files (or share them, on a phone).

4 · Stress test

Generates a synthetic song with seven tracks of the chosen length and loads it, to see how much audio this device can hold. If the page reloads by itself, the test went past the limit; the report says so.

5 · Engine speed

Runs the engine as fast as it can on 20 seconds of the loaded song with all tracks on, at the current tempo and key. Real-time playback needs at least 1×; 3× or more leaves a comfortable margin. It also reports the slowest moments, a single audio block and a stretch of about 23 ms, against the time playback gives them: crackles come from those moments, not from the average. Close to 100% or above means a risk of crackles.

6 · Separation (computer)

Separates a song into the six stems inside the browser, with the model of EUTERIA (Python), BS-RoFormer SW, running on the graphics card. Chrome or Edge on a computer. The model is downloaded once from Hugging Face and kept by the browser.

This needs Chrome or Edge on a computer with WebGPU.

"Separate the open song" uses the Original Track of the song opened in card 1. If that song already has stems from EUTERIA (Python), the new ones join the track list next to them ("Vocals · web" and so on) and the report says how close each pair is. "Compare runtimes" times the first blocks of the open song with each runtime, estimates how long the whole song would take, profiles the graphics card with ONNX Runtime 1.24, and selects the fastest runtime.
